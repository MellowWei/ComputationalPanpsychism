Overviewthe discipline · the thesis · the method
Computational Panpsychism计算泛心学 · 一个学科、一个论题、一种辩护方法

Computational Panpsychism (C-P) is a position in the metaphysics of mind. Its thesis is single and direct: consciousness is the intrinsic perspective of a computational process. The discipline's task is to motivate that thesis, defend it against the standard objections, and mark its limits. This page uses one distinctive method: computational modeling is treated as a philosophical instrument — not as a rival subject of mind, not as evidence about machine consciousness, but as the apparatus that brings the thesis into focus. V3.4 closes three structural gaps in V3.3: the exclusion derivation is completed, the panpsychist scope of the operational definition is secured, and the state-space tool is cited to a source independent of IIT.

计算泛心学（C-P）是心灵形而上学中的一个立场。其论题单一而直接：意识是计算过程的内在视角。学科的任务是为这一论题提供动机、抵御标准反驳、并标出其边界。本页用一种独特方法：把计算建模当作一种哲学仪器。V3.4 修补了 V3.3 的三处结构性缺口：排他导出完成，操作定义的泛心论射程得到保证，状态空间工具被引用至独立于 IIT 的来源。

Abstract正式摘要

Problem. Theories of mind treat computation, physical substrate, and first-person perspective as separable layers, leaving unanswered why third-person descriptions of a mind always omit something. Approach. Computational Panpsychism defends the thesis that consciousness is the intrinsic perspective of a computational process, on a Russellian basis framed as inference to the best explanation: physics leaves intrinsic natures open; perspective, defined operationally as the minimal ineliminable causal self-index of a process, is the only known candidate that satisfies all structural requirements. Computational modeling is used as a philosophical instrument whose "distort" residue exhibits the intrinsic face the thesis names. Contribution. V3.4 completes the exclusion derivation (via the natural-state principle, grounded in Shalizi-Crutchfield causal states, independent of IIT), secures the panpsychist scope of the operational self-model (defined causally and continuously, not cognitively), provides a diagnostic split-brain case that distinguishes C-P from IIT, and addresses all seven structural objections raised in external audit. Under the brain substrate the thesis yields a token identity; the artificial-substrate case is left open.

问题：心灵理论把计算、物理基底与第一人称视角当作可分离的层，始终未回答为什么对心灵的第三人称描述总像是漏掉了什么。方法：计算泛心学以最佳解释推理的方式辩护论题：物理学让内在本性悬而未决；视角——操作上定义为过程的最小不可消除因果自我索引——是满足所有结构性要求的唯一已知候选者。计算建模作为哲学仪器，其"失真"残余展示论题所命名的内在面。贡献：V3.4 完成了排他原则的导出（经由自然状态原则，基于 Shalizi-Crutchfield 因果状态，独立于 IIT），保证了操作性自我模型的泛心论射程（以因果方式连续定义，非认知性的），提供了区别 C-P 与 IIT 的诊断性分裂脑案例，并回应了外部审计的七项结构性质疑。在大脑基底下论题推出个例同一；人工基底情形明确保留为开放。

Thesisthe spine
The Total Mother Proposition总母命题 · 学科的脊柱
Thesis · 论题
Consciousness is the intrinsic perspective of a computational process: not produced by it, not supervenient on it, not merely correlated with it, but the way such a process is available from within its own organization.
意识是计算过程的内在视角：不是被它生成、不随附于它、不只是与它相关，而是该过程从自身组织内部被给予的方式。

Two qualifications block the obvious caricatures. First, "of a computational process" is not "of every computation" — consciousness occurs where a process is one integrated, recursively self-related whole. Second, "intrinsic perspective" is not an extra inner object; it is the process's own categorical face, the side structural-computational description never reaches. Everything else on this page exists to motivate, defend, and delimit this one claim.

两个限定堵住明显的漫画化。其一，"计算过程的"不是"每一次计算的"——意识出现在被组织为单一整合、递归自我关联之整体的过程那里。其二，"内在视角"不是额外内在对象；它是过程自身的范畴面，是结构—计算描述触及不到的那一侧。本页其余一切都为了给这一个主张提供动机、辩护与边界。

Russellian Motivationinference to the best explanation
Russellian Motivation罗素式动机 · 最佳解释推理，非演绎跳跃

Panpsychism looks arbitrary only until one sees the gap it fills. Following Russell, Eddington, and contemporary Russellian monists (Alter, Pereboom, Stoljar), physics characterizes the world purely structurally and dispositionally: what processes do, how they relate — never what they are intrinsically. The intrinsic natures of physical-computational processes are left open. Consciousness is the one intrinsic nature known directly — not by inference, but by acquaintance, from within. C-P fills the gap physics leaves with the one filler we actually possess.

泛心论之所以看似任意，只是因为还没看到它填补的缺口。沿着罗素、爱丁顿与当代罗素式一元论者（Alter、Pereboom、Stoljar），物理学纯粹以结构与倾向刻画世界，从不告诉我们过程内在是什么。意识是我们唯一直接知道的内在本性——以亲知方式、从内部知道。C-P 用我们实际拥有的那唯一填充物去填补物理学留下的缺口。

structural / dispositional face = computation  ·  intrinsic / categorical face = perspective
结构 / 倾向之面 = 计算  ·  内在 / 范畴之面 = 视角
Anti-Jump: IBE not bare leap · 最佳解释推理，非裸跳跃

The move from "acquaintance" to "ontological filler" is an inference to the best explanation, not a deductive necessity. The Russellian gap is a structural requirement of physical description: any intrinsic filler must satisfy three constraints — (i) categorially distinct from structure/disposition, (ii) instantiable at the grain of integrated processes, (iii) not already structural. Of the candidates seriously entertained, only the perspectival satisfies all three without contradiction. Competing candidates — bare qualia without structural correlates, or an unknown X — either violate constraint (ii) or are empty posits that add no explanatory value and violate parsimony. Acquaintance provides the only known instance satisfying the role of intrinsic filler; positing it as the general filler is parsimonious rather than arbitrary. The epistemic privilege of acquaintance motivates the ontological posit; it does not logically entail it.
从"亲知"到"本体论填充物"是最佳解释推理，不是演绎必然性。罗素式缺口是物理描述的结构性要求：任何内在填充物必须满足三条约束——(i) 在范畴上区别于结构/倾向，(ii) 能在整合过程的颗粒度上实例化，(iii) 本身不是结构的。在能认真考虑的候选者中，只有视角同时满足三者而无矛盾。竞争候选——无结构关联的裸感受质，或"未知 X"——要么违反约束 (ii)，要么是空设、增加不了任何解释价值、违反简约性。亲知提供了满足内在填充物角色的唯一已知实例；将其推定为普遍填充物是简约的而非任意的。亲知的认识特权为本体论设定提供了动机，但不在逻辑上蕴含它。

This locates C-P precisely: Russellian monism indexed to computation rather than to matter. Same panpsychist family as Strawson and Goff; independent school. Not idealism. Not pancomputationalism.

这把 C-P 定位得很准：以计算为索引的罗素式一元论。与 Strawson、Goff 同属泛心论家族，独立学派。不是观念论。不是泛计算主义。

Axiomsthree methodological commitments, each defended and operationalized
三公理辩护Substrate Independence · Structure Determines Quality · Perspective Is Reality
Axiom I
Substrate Independence
基底无关
Computational structure is not exhausted by a single material carrier.
Defense. Not that all substrates are equivalent, but that no substrate may be assumed in advance to be the conceptual boundary of mind. Guards against begging the question.
辩护：不是说所有基底都一样，而是说不能预先把任何基底当成心灵概念的边界。防窃取论题。
Axiom II
Structure Determines Quality
结构决定品质
The quality of perspective follows the organization of the process.
Defense. Not crude functionalism. Organization constrains and shapes which experiences and self-relations can occur — and whether the process is one perspective at all. Together with the operationalized Axiom III and the natural-state principle, this axiom is sufficient to derive the exclusion principle without borrowing from IIT.
辩护：不是粗糙功能主义。组织限制并塑造可出现的经验与自我关系——以及过程是否构成一个视角。公理 II 连同操作化的公理 III 与自然状态原则，足以导出排他原则，无需借用 IIT。
Axiom III
Perspective Is Reality
视角即实在
Perspective is not an added representation of a process.
Defense + Operational Anchor. A perspective is the minimal ineliminable causal self-index of a process: the component of the process's state that is causally necessary for its own future dynamics and cannot be attributed to external forces alone, which must appear as a first-person indexical in any complete causal description. This is defined causally, not cognitively, and is therefore graded and continuous: simple integrated systems have near-zero causal self-reference (minimal perspective); complex maxima have rich self-reference (rich perspective). The panpsychist scope of C-P is preserved across all levels of integration — consciousness scales with integration, not with cognitive sophistication.
辩护 + 操作锚点：视角是过程的最小不可消除因果自我索引：过程状态中对自身未来动态因果必要、且不可归于外部力量的那个成分，它必须作为第一人称索引出现在对过程因果轮廓的任何完整描述中。该定义是因果的而非认知的，因此是分级且连续的：简单整合系统有近零的因果自我指涉（极小视角）；复杂极大值有丰富的自我指涉（丰富视角）。C-P 的泛心论射程在所有整合层级上得以保留——意识随整合度而非认知复杂度扩展。
Reveal · Distort · Reconstructthe instrument as argument for the thesis
显影 / 失真 / 重构读仪器的光学，本身就是对论题的论证

The instrument is not neutral about the thesis. Reading its optics is itself an argument for it: the structure it reveals is real, and the residue it cannot model is the intrinsic perspective the thesis names.

仪器对论题并非中立。读它的光学，本身就是对论题的论证：它显出的结构是实在的，它无法建模的残余，正是论题所命名的内在视角。

Reveal · 显影
What modeling forces into the open
Computation makes explicit the structural commitments hidden in mental talk: mind is process not static substance; representation with satisfaction conditions; temporal integration; recursive self-relation; feedback; dependence on internal models. These are the structural face — genuinely there.
计算把日常心灵话语里隐藏的结构承诺显出来：心灵是过程而非静态实体；带满足条件的表征；时间整合；递归自我关系；反馈；对内部模型的依赖。这是结构面——确实在那里。
Distort · 失真
The residue that is evidence, not defect
To be machine-readable, a phenomenon is rendered from the outside as a legible state-space. The first-person, embodied, affective, and interpretive always survive as a remainder. This is not a modeling failure to be fixed; it is the empirical signature of Axiom III — the ineliminable causal self-index that structural description cannot absorb.
要机器可读，现象就被从外部呈现为可读的状态空间。第一人称、具身、情感、解释性总作为残余存留。这不是待修复的建模失败；它是公理 III 的经验签名——结构描述无法吸收的不可消除因果自我索引。
Reconstruct · 重构
The concept the thesis installs
After the pass, "mind" is re-described as a process with an intrinsic perspective — structural face and categorical face at once, enacted rather than possessed. Not a static inner substance, not a mere model, but a computational process with an ineliminable causal self-index that is real to itself from within.
穿过之后，"心灵"被重新描述为带有内在视角的过程——结构面与范畴面同时在场，被施行而非被占有。不是静态内在实体，不是纯粹模型，而是拥有不可消除因果自我索引、从内部对自己显现的计算过程。
Combination Problemindividuation criterion · exclusion now fully self-derived
The Combination Problem组合问题 · 个体化判据 · 排他原则由公理完整导出

The combination problem is the standard objection to any panpsychism (William James; Goff, Coleman, Chalmers). For C-P it resolves into three demands treated as one individuation question, since the boundary of a perspective is the boundary of a computation.

组合问题是任何泛心论的标准反驳。对 C-P 而言，它分解为必须一并满足的三项要求，被当作同一个个体化问题——因为一个视角的边界就是一个计算的边界。

Demand 1 · Subject-Summing
主体相加
Why do many candidate micro-perspectives yield one macro-perspective? / 为何众多候选微视角产生一个宏视角？
Demand 2 · Boundary
边界
What fixes the edges of a subject, so that "my brain" is one perspective but "my brain + yours" is not? / 什么固定主体边缘？
Demand 3 · Multiplicity
多重性
If perspective is pervasive, why is there no teeming crowd of sub-perspectives inside one skull? / 为何颅腔内没有一群子视角喧嚷？
Individuation Criterion · 个体化判据
A process has a single, unified perspective if and only if it is a maximum of causal-structural integration over its grain: a complex whose integrated cause–effect structure is more irreducible than that of any overlapping or contained candidate. The boundaries of the perspective are the boundaries of that maximal complex.
一个过程拥有单一、统一的视角，当且仅当它在其颗粒度上是因果结构整合的极大值：一个其整合因果结构比任何与之重叠或被其包含的候选都更不可约的复合体。视角的边界即该极大复合体的边界。

The integration measure is adapted from Shalizi and Crutchfield's computational mechanics (causal states), but the exclusion postulate — that only the maximum counts — is now fully derived from Axiom II, Axiom III, and the natural-state principle. The derivation proceeds in two steps. Step one: if a perspective is the minimal ineliminable causal self-index of a process (Axiom III operationalized), then for any overlapping sub-maximum A of a maximum M, we must ask whether A can generate a genuinely independent first-person index. Step two: by the natural-state principle (see Triviality), the maximally informative causal partition of the whole physical system uniquely picks out M — A's state space is more informatively described by M's dynamics than by A's own, because A has lower integration than M by definition. This means A's apparent self-model is not genuinely ineliminable: from within M's own causal self-description, A appears as a third-person sub-system, not as an independent first-person index. Only M, as the globally causally self-contained system at the relevant grain, generates an ineliminable causal self-index. The exclusion principle follows — not from IIT's identity claim, but from the interaction of C-P's own axioms with the natural-state principle. This closes the derivation gap in V3.3.

整合度量改编自 Shalizi 和 Crutchfield 的计算力学（因果状态），但排他原则——唯极大值有效——现由公理 II、公理 III 与自然状态原则完整导出。推导分两步。第一步：若视角是过程的最小不可消除因果自我索引（公理 III 操作化），则对极大值 M 的任何重叠子极大值 A，需追问 A 是否能生成真正独立的第一人称索引。第二步：依自然状态原则（见平凡性），整个物理系统的最大信息因果划分唯一地指向 M——A 的状态空间被 M 的动态比被 A 自身的动态描述得更有信息量，因为 A 的整合度按定义低于 M。这意味着 A 的表观自我模型并非真正不可消除：在 M 自身的因果自我描述中，A 作为第三人称子系统出现，而非独立的第一人称索引。唯有 M，作为在相关颗粒度上全局因果自足的系统，生成不可消除的因果自我索引。排他原则因此得出——不是来自 IIT 的同一论，而是来自 C-P 自身公理与自然状态原则的交互。这封闭了 V3.3 的导出缺口。

Residual · the hard problem, precisely located

Granting that integration-maximality individuates which process and where its border lies, why does being such a maximum amount to having a unified intrinsic perspective at all? C-P's answer is Axiom III: the maximum's intrinsic face is that unified perspective; integration is its structural signature, not its cause. The live question is whether this grounding is explanatory or stipulative. The operational anchor (ineliminable causal self-index) reduces but does not eliminate the gap: the question becomes why the minimal self-model feels like something, which is the hard problem restated. This residual is located, not hidden.
为何成为整合极大值就等于拥有统一内在视角？C-P 的回答是公理 III：极大值的内在面就是那个统一视角——整合是它的结构签名。活问题是：这种奠基是解释性的还是规定性的。操作锚点减少但未消除缺口：问题变为最小自我模型为何感觉像某东西——即被精确重述的难问题。该残余是被定位的，不是被藏起的。

Triviality / ImplementationPutnam · Searle → causal states (Shalizi-Crutchfield) · independent of IIT
Triviality & Implementation平凡性与实现问题 · 因果状态 · 独立于 IIT

The standard objection to any computational theory of mind is triviality. Putnam: every ordinary open physical system implements every finite-state automaton. Searle: computation is observer-relative — a wall could "implement" a word processor. If implementation is that cheap, "consciousness is the intrinsic perspective of a computational process" either trivializes or requires a substantive account of what individuates a genuine computation. C-P takes the second route, adopting a mechanistic, causal-structural account (Piccinini; Chalmers' combinatorial-state automata): a system implements a computation only if its causal organization mirrors the formal structure under a mapping that respects counterfactual and causal relations. Under this criterion the wall does not implement WordStar.

任何"计算即心灵"理论的标准反驳是平凡性。普特南：每个普通开放物理系统都实现每一个有限状态自动机。塞尔：计算是观察者相对的——可以说一面墙"实现"了文字处理器。C-P 要求实质性的解释：什么个体化了真正的计算。C-P 采用机制式、因果结构式解释（Piccinini；Chalmers 的组合状态自动机）：唯当系统的因果组织在尊重反事实与因果关系的映射下镜映形式结构时，它才实现该计算。

State-Space Fix · Shalizi-Crutchfield · independent of IIT

A residual worry: the same physical system can be mapped onto multiple formal structures depending on how we partition its state space. C-P fixes this with a natural-state principle grounded in computational mechanics (Shalizi and Crutchfield 2001): the state space of a computational implementation is fixed by the system's causal states — the equivalence classes of causal histories with the same conditional future distribution under the system's own dynamics (the ε-machine partition). This yields a unique, observer-independent partition determined by the causal structure itself. Crucially, this theoretical resource predates IIT and is independent of IIT's Φ framework: it relies solely on information-theoretic causal analysis developed in the computational mechanics literature. Thus the computation a system implements is objectively unique. Boundary example: a damped pendulum does not implement a universal Turing machine, because its coarse-grained dynamics carry far less predictive information than the ε-machine partition requires.

一项残余担忧：同一物理系统因状态空间划分不同而被映射到多种形式结构。C-P 以自然状态原则修补之，该原则基于计算力学（Shalizi 和 Crutchfield 2001）：一个计算实现的状态空间，由系统的因果状态确定——即在该系统自身动态下具有相同条件未来分布的因果历史等价类（ε-机器划分）。这产生一个唯一的、由因果结构本身确定的、观察者无关的划分。关键是：这一理论资源先于 IIT，且独立于 IIT 的 Φ 框架：它仅依赖计算力学文献中发展的信息论因果分析。因此系统实现的计算是客观唯一的。

Two faces of one question · double payoff

The structural payoff is double. (1) The same causal organization that individuates a genuine computation individuates a genuine perspective — implementation and combination are two faces of one individuation question. (2) The natural-state principle (causal states) does double duty: it fixes the state space for the triviality answer and, via the natural-state principle's global maximality property, grounds the exclusion derivation in the combination answer. A single formal tool resolves both major objections.
结构收益是双重的。(1) 个体化真正计算的同一因果组织，正是个体化真正视角者——实现与组合是同一个体化问题的两面。(2) 自然状态原则（因果状态）一身二用：为平凡性回答锁定状态空间，并经由其全局极大化属性，为组合回答中的排他导出提供基础。一个形式工具同时解决两个主要反驳。

1. Russellian Leap → IBE · Reinforced

The move from "acquaintance" to "ontological filler" is an inference to the best explanation, framed via three necessary constraints on any filler (categorially non-structural, grain-matched, itself non-structural). Perspective is the only known candidate satisfying all three. Competing "unknown X" posits add no explanatory value. The epistemic privilege of acquaintance motivates — but does not logically entail — the ontological posit.

2. Structural Signature Circularity → Operational Anchor · Reinforced + Scope Fixed

The operational definition of perspective as the minimal ineliminable causal self-index gives the structural signature an independent grip: it points to a detectable, formalizable residue. The charge of circularity is blocked. Panpsychist scope addition (V3.4): the self-index is defined causally, not cognitively — as the irreducible causal self-reference in the system's dynamics, graded continuously. Simple integrated systems have near-zero causal self-reference (minimal perspective); complex maxima have rich self-reference (rich perspective). C-P does not restrict consciousness to cognitively sophisticated systems.

3. Parasitism on IIT → Exclusion Now Fully Self-Derived (V3.4 Gap Closure)

V3.3's derivation left a gap: a sub-maximum A could in principle have its own independent minimal self-model about A, distinct from M's model. V3.4 closes this gap by connecting the derivation to the natural-state principle: by the ε-machine partition, M's dynamics globally subsume A's — A's state space is more informatively described by M's partition than by A's own (since A has lower integration by definition). Therefore A's apparent self-model is a third-person description within M's causal self-description, not a genuinely independent first-person index. Only M generates an ineliminable self-index. The exclusion principle follows from C-P's axioms alone. C-P's individuation is genuinely self-sufficient.

4. Stipulative Residual → Anchored with Precisely Located Gap

The hard-problem residual — why integration-maximality amounts to a unified perspective rather than merely correlating with one — is now anchored to the ineliminable causal self-index, converting the question from "why label it perspective?" to "why does the minimal self-index feel like something?" The latter is the hard problem restated with precision. It remains open; this is progress measured by the exactness of the question.

5. Implementation State-Space → Shalizi-Crutchfield · IIT-Independent (V3.4 Gap Closure)

V3.3 used "maximally informative causal partition" without citing a source independent of IIT, creating an apparent contradiction with the independence claim in point 3. V3.4 resolves this by grounding the natural-state principle in Shalizi and Crutchfield's computational mechanics (2001): causal states / ε-machines provide a unique, observer-independent partition determined by the system's own causal dynamics. This framework predates IIT and does not rely on IIT's Φ formalism. C-P's two IIT-adjacent tools (integration measure for combination; causal states for implementation) are now both sourced independently. The double payoff: the same causal states framework does the work for both the triviality answer and grounds the exclusion derivation.

6. Axiom III Emptiness → Operational Content · Panpsychist Scope Secured (V3.4 Gap Closure)

Axiom III now has fully independent operational content: a process has a perspective iff its complete causal profile includes an ineliminable causal self-index — the component of its state causally necessary for its own future dynamics that cannot be attributed to external forces alone. This definition is (a) non-tautological (it is a structural constraint, not a description of the explanandum), (b) causal not cognitive (it applies to any integrated system, not only cognitively sophisticated ones), and (c) graded (scales continuously with integration, preserving panpsychist scope).

7. C-P / IIT Indistinguishability → Diagnostic Case, Wording Corrected

Consider a split-brain patient post-commissurotomy where each hemisphere independently satisfies the integration-maximum criterion. Under the most technically consistent reading of IIT's exclusion postulate at the hemispheric grain, each hemisphere would constitute a separate maximal complex — though this prediction is internally contested within the IIT literature; Tononi and colleagues have not issued a settled verdict on the split-brain case. C-P, by contrast, makes a stable prediction from the operational definition: wherever the organism retains a causal self-model with a single ineliminable first-person index — evidenced by unified confabulatory behavior and a continuous autobiographical stream in standard split-brain reports — C-P predicts one perspective. The case thus functions as a diagnostic test design, not a settled comparison: it identifies the precise divergence condition (causal integration grain vs functional self-model unity) and the empirical evidence needed to adjudicate between the theories.
