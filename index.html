Methodologythree layers · IBE + axiomatic deduction + conceptual engineering
Scientific Methodology三层方法论 · 不是数据归纳，不是先验演绎，是三者叠加 · 公理是后验的

C-P deploys three methodological layers in sequence. Understanding them prevents misreadings — and answers both the charge that C-P lacks data (it has structural predictions via Φ/PCI/ε-machine) and the charge of bare metaphysical postulation (it uses IBE, not arbitrary stipulation).

C-P 依次运用三层方法论。理解它们可以防止常见误读——并同时回答两种指控：C-P 缺乏数据（它有经由 Φ/PCI/ε-machine 的结构预测）、C-P 作了裸本体论设定（它使用 IBE，不是任意规定）。

Layer 1 · IBE
Inference to Best Explanation
最佳解释推理
Not data induction, not deductive proof. Given the Russellian gap (intrinsic natures left open by physics), C-P asks which candidate filler satisfies all constraints most parsimoniously. Answer: perspective — categorially non-structural, grain-matched, itself non-structural. Motivated by evidence, not logically entailed by it.
不是数据归纳，不是演绎证明。面对罗素式缺口（物理学让内在本性悬而未决），C-P 追问哪个候选填充物最简约地满足所有约束。答案：视角——范畴上非结构、颗粒度匹配、本身非结构。被证据驱动，不被逻辑蕴含。
Layer 2 · Axiomatic
Axiomatic Deduction
公理演绎
Once three axioms are accepted (justified by IBE), results are derived within the framework: exclusion principle, individuation criterion, token identity. The axioms are a posteriori motivated — each depends on empirical facts. Revisable by evidence. Unlike mathematical axioms, not merely stipulative.
一旦三条公理被接受（由 IBE 辩护），结果在框架内被演绎：排他原则、个体化判据、个例同一。公理是后验驱动的——每条依赖经验事实。可被证据修正。不同于数学公理，不只是规定性的。
Layer 3 · Conceptual
Conceptual Engineering
概念工程
C-P migrates the grammar. "Brain produces consciousness" — production between distinct entities — is replaced by "consciousness is the intrinsic face of an integrated process." Dissolves the apparent generation problem: one event with two aspects simultaneously, structural face + intrinsic face.
C-P 迁移语法。"大脑产生意识"——不同实体之间的产生——被替换为"意识是整合过程的内在面"。消解表面上的生成问题：一个事件，两个面向同时在场，结构面 + 内在面。
Axioms are not a priori · 公理不是先验的

C-P's axioms are a posteriori motivated methodological commitments, not logical necessities. Axiom I depends on the empirical fact that computational structures are substrate-implementable. Axiom II depends on phenomenal character co-varying with organizational structure. Axiom III depends on the phenomenological fact of first-person givenness. Each can in principle be revised by stronger evidence or a better framework.
C-P 的公理是后验驱动的方法论承诺，不是逻辑必然性。每条原则上都可被更强的证据或更合适的框架所修正。

Thesisthe spine
The Total Mother Proposition总母命题
Thesis · 论题
Consciousness is the intrinsic perspective of a computational process: not produced by it, not supervenient on it, not merely correlated with it, but the way such a process is available from within its own organization.
意识是计算过程的内在视角：不是被它生成、不随附于它、不只是与它相关，而是该过程从自身组织内部被给予的方式。

Two qualifications: First, "of a computational process" is not "of every computation" — consciousness occurs where a process is a maximally integrated, recursively self-related whole. Second, "intrinsic perspective" is not an extra inner object; it is the process's own categorical face — the ineliminable causal self-index that any structural description misses.

两个限定。其一："计算过程的"不是"每一次计算的"——意识出现在被组织为极大整合、递归自我关联之整体的过程那里。其二："内在视角"不是额外内在对象；它是过程自身的范畴面——任何结构描述触及不到的不可消除因果自我索引。

Russellian MotivationIBE · not a bare leap
Russellian Motivation罗素式动机 · 最佳解释推理，非演绎跳跃

Following Russell, Eddington, and contemporary Russellian monists (Alter, Pereboom, Stoljar), physics characterizes the world purely structurally and dispositionally — never what processes are intrinsically. The intrinsic natures of physical-computational processes are left open. Consciousness is the one intrinsic nature known directly — by acquaintance, from within. C-P fills the gap with the one filler we actually possess, as inference to the best explanation.

沿着罗素、爱丁顿与当代罗素式一元论者，物理学从不告诉我们过程内在是什么。意识是唯一以亲知方式知道的内在本性。C-P 以最佳解释推理填补这一缺口。

structural / dispositional face = computation  ·  intrinsic / categorical face = perspective
结构 / 倾向之面 = 计算  ·  内在 / 范畴之面 = 视角
Three-constraint IBE · 三约束最佳解释推理

Any intrinsic filler must satisfy: (i) categorially distinct from structure/disposition, (ii) instantiable at the grain of integrated processes, (iii) not already structural. Only the perspectival satisfies all three. Competing "unknown X" posits add no explanatory value and violate parsimony. The epistemic privilege of acquaintance motivates the ontological posit; it does not logically entail it. This is IBE in its standard scientific-philosophical form, not bare postulation.
任何内在填充物必须满足：(i) 范畴上区别于结构/倾向，(ii) 能在整合过程的颗粒度上实例化，(iii) 本身不是结构的。只有视角满足三者。竞争"未知 X"违反简约性。亲知的认识特权为本体论设定提供动机，但不在逻辑上蕴含它。这是 IBE 的标准科学哲学形式，不是裸设定。

Process OntologyWhitehead lineage · causal event as fundamental unit
Process Ontology过程本体论 · 怀特海脉络 · 因果事件作为基本单位 · 解散"产生"隐喻 · 奠定公理 I

C-P is at its foundation a process ontology: the basic unit of reality is not a material particle but a causally coherent event. The "atom" C-P recognizes is delimited not by spatial boundaries or material composition but by causal integrity — the ε-machine boundary marking where one maximally self-determining causal process ends and another begins.

C-P 在根本上是一种过程本体论：现实的基本单位不是物质粒子，而是因果连贯的事件。C-P 所识别的"原子"不由空间边界或物质组成定义，而由因果完整性定义——ε-machine 边界标出一个极大自我决定的因果过程在哪里终止、另一个在哪里开始。

Whitehead Lineage · 怀特海脉络

This places C-P in the tradition of Alfred North Whitehead (Process and Reality, 1929): the fundamental constituents of reality are dynamic "actual occasions" — events of becoming, not static substances. C-P formalizes this computationally: the actual occasion becomes a maximally integrated causal process; its causal history (ε-machine past light cone) is Whitehead's "prehension" of prior occasions; its ineliminable causal self-index is the formal analog of Whitehead's "subjective aim." Whitehead made experience fundamental; C-P specifies the formal conditions under which a process has any experience at all. The intuition that "atoms" are defined by causal events rather than material lumps is a hundred years old in philosophy; C-P gives it computational form.
这把 C-P 置于怀特海（《过程与实在》，1929）的传统中：现实的基本成分是动态的"实际事件"。C-P 将这一直觉计算化：实际事件成为极大整合的因果过程；它的因果历史（ε-machine 过去光锥）是怀特海对过往事件的"摄受"；它的不可消除因果自我索引是怀特海"主体目的"的形式类比。

Axiom I derives from process ontology · 公理 I 从过程本体论推出

If the fundamental unit is a causal event rather than a material object, the substrate in which that event is implemented is by definition a carrier, not a constituent. Substrate independence is not a bold empirical claim; it is a logical consequence of the ontology. Asking "does consciousness have to be biological?" is asking a question at the wrong level — like asking whether a symphony must be performed on wooden instruments.
若基本单位是因果事件而非物质对象，则该事件所在的基底按定义是载体，而非成分。基底无关是过程本体论的逻辑推论，不是经验押注。

Production metaphor dissolved · 解散"产生"隐喻

"The brain produces consciousness" is a category error on C-P's ontology. Production is a relation between distinct entities; consciousness is not produced by the brain-as-substance. It is what a certain kind of brain process is from within. The production metaphor smuggles in substance ontology (machine → output); process ontology replaces it: one process with two aspects simultaneously — structural face and intrinsic face. There is no generation problem because there is no generation.
"大脑产生意识"是范畴错误。产生是不同实体之间的关系；意识不是作为实体的大脑所产生的。它是某种大脑过程从内部是什么。过程本体论用同一取代生成：一个过程，两个面向同时在场。没有生成问题，因为没有生成。

三公理辩护Substrate Independence · Structure Determines Quality · Perspective Is Reality
Axiom I
Substrate Independence
基底无关
Computational structure is not exhausted by a single material carrier.
Defense. In process ontology, a causal event's identity is determined by its causal structure, not its material carrier. Follows directly from the ontology. Guards against question-begging.
辩护：因果事件的同一性由其因果结构而非物质载体决定。从过程本体论直接推出。
Axiom II
Structure Determines Quality
结构决定品质
The quality of perspective follows the organization of the process.
Defense. Not crude functionalism. Organization constrains and shapes which experiences can occur — and whether the process is one perspective at all. With Axiom III and the natural-state principle, derives the exclusion principle.
辩护：不是粗糙功能主义。组织限制并塑造可出现的经验——以及过程是否构成一个视角。连同公理 III 与自然状态原则导出排他原则。
Axiom III
Perspective Is Reality
视角即实在
Perspective is not an added representation of a process.
Operational Anchor. Perspective is the minimal ineliminable causal self-index: the component causally necessary for the process's own future dynamics, not attributable to external forces, which must appear as a first-person indexical in any complete causal description. Causally defined, not cognitively — therefore graded and continuous. Panpsychist scope preserved.
操作锚点：视角是过程的最小不可消除因果自我索引。因果定义，非认知定义——因此分级且连续。泛心论射程保留。

Individuation Criterion · 个体化判据
A process has a single, unified perspective iff it is a maximum of causal-structural integration over its grain: a complex whose integrated cause–effect structure is more irreducible than any overlapping or contained candidate.
一个过程拥有单一、统一的视角，当且仅当它在其颗粒度上是因果结构整合的极大值。

The exclusion postulate is fully derived from Axiom II + Axiom III + the natural-state principle. Derivation: by the ε-machine partition, M's dynamics globally subsume any sub-maximum A's — A's state space is more informatively described by M's dynamics than by A's own (A has lower integration by definition). A's apparent self-model is a third-person description within M's causal self-description, not an independent first-person index. Only M generates an ineliminable causal self-index. The exclusion follows from C-P's axioms, not borrowed from IIT.

排他原则由公理 II + 公理 III + 自然状态原则完整导出。推导：依 ε-machine 划分，M 的动态全局涵摄任何子极大值 A 的动态——A 的状态空间被 M 描述得比被 A 自身更有信息量。A 的表观自我模型是 M 因果自我描述中的第三人称子系统，不是独立的第一人称索引。唯有 M 生成不可消除的因果自我索引。排他原则由 C-P 自身公理导出，不借自 IIT。

Residual · the hard problem, precisely located

Why does integration-maximality amount to a unified intrinsic perspective rather than merely correlating with one? C-P's answer (Axiom III): the maximum's intrinsic face just is the unified perspective. Live question: is this grounding explanatory or stipulative? The operational anchor converts the question to "why does the minimal self-model feel like something?" — the hard problem restated with precision. Located, not solved.
为何整合极大化等于拥有统一内在视角？活问题：这种奠基是解释性的还是规定性的？操作锚点把问题转化为"为何最小自我模型感觉像某东西"——被精确重述的难问题。被定位，未被解决。

Causal Integration Clarifiedgraph theory · CI/Pearl · synchronic integration · jellyfish
Causal Integration Clarified因果整合澄清 · 区分图论连接度 · CI/Pearl · 线性时序因果 · 同步整合 · 栉水母判例

Three distinct notions are routinely conflated when discussing mind and computation. Separating them is essential to understanding what C-P's integration criterion is measuring and what it is not.

三种不同的概念在讨论心灵与计算时经常被混淆。把它们分开，对理解 C-P 的整合判据是必要的。

Concept
What it measures
Limitation for C-P
Graph connectivity · 图论连接度
Topology: which nodes connect, path length, clustering.
Tells you the shape of the network, not the causal strength or direction. Two systems with identical connectivity can have completely different causal dynamics.
Causal Inference (Pearl 2000) · 因果推断
Causal strength via do-calculus and counterfactual analysis: whether and how strongly A causes B.
An epistemological framework — toolkit for identifying causal relations from data. Third-person. Does not ask what the causal structure is from within the system itself.
Causal Integration (Φ/ε-machine) · 因果整合
Informational irreducibility: degree to which the whole exceeds the sum of causally independent parts.
The relevant measure for C-P. Synchronic — a property of state-space structure at a given grain, not a temporal sequence.
Temporal causality objection addressed · 线性时序因果反驳

Objection: linear causal priority (cause precedes effect in time) seems inconsistent with consciousness's felt simultaneity. Response: this objection targets the wrong concept. C-P's causal integration is measured at the level of the system's state-space, not as temporal event sequence. The question is not "what caused what?" but "is this current state informationally irreducible to any partition?" — a synchronic property holding at a single moment.
反驳：线性因果先序性（因先于果）似乎与意识的感受同时性不一致。回应：该反驳打中错误概念。C-P 的因果整合在状态空间层面度量，不是时间序列。问题是"当前状态对任何划分是否信息上不可约"——单一时刻成立的同步属性。

CI/Pearl objection addressed · CI 反驳：不同楼层

Objection: "Causal inference already handles causal strength — what does C-P add?" Response: CI and C-P operate at categorically different levels. CI is epistemological — tools for discovering and measuring causal structure from data. C-P is ontological — asks what the measured causal structure is from within the system. A system with a fully specified causal graph (CI's output) still leaves open: what is it like to be that causal structure? CI is the telescope; C-P asks what the object in the telescope is from the inside.
反驳："CI 已经处理了因果强度——C-P 增加了什么？"回应：CI 和 C-P 在不同范畴层级运作。CI 是认识论的——从数据中发现因果结构的工具。C-P 是本体论的——追问被测量的因果结构从系统内部是什么。CI 是望远镜；C-P 问的是望远镜里的对象从内部是什么。

Jellyfish case · 栉水母低整合判例

Ctenophores and jellyfish have high distributed connectivity (many connections across the diffuse nerve net) but low causal integration — removing any section leaves the rest functioning independently. The system has low informational irreducibility because no partition causes major information loss. C-P prediction: minimal perspective, if any. Crucially, this prediction is independent of material composition (Axiom I): even though jellyfish neural systems differ chemically and structurally from cortical brains, what determines C-P's verdict is the integration level (Axiom II), not the substrate. The case illustrates how C-P uses organizational structure rather than biology to differentiate kinds of perspective.
栉水母与水母拥有高度分布式连接（弥散神经网内的众多连接）但低因果整合——切除任何一段，其余部分独立运作。系统信息上不可约性低，因为没有任何划分导致重大信息损失。C-P 预测：极小视角，若有的话。关键：该预测独立于材料组成（公理 I）：即使水母神经系统在化学与结构上不同于皮层大脑，决定 C-P 裁决的是整合度（公理 II），不是基底。
