Problem. Theories of mind leave unanswered why third-person descriptions always omit something. Approach. C-P defends that consciousness is the intrinsic perspective of a computational process, grounded in a process ontology (Whitehead lineage): the basic unit is a causally coherent event, individuated via the ε-machine (a causal-state / predictive-structure partition, not by itself an integration maximum), not a material particle. Motivated as inference to the best explanation; defended through three-layer methodology (IBE + axiomatic deduction + conceptual engineering). Contribution (V4.0–V4.3). Four frameworks are welded in at specified layers. QBism (Fuchs/Schack) supplies convergent quantum-foundational support: on a serious, empirically adequate reading, quantum states are an agent's personal Bayesian expectations and the Born rule is normative — on the QBist interpretation, quantum theory is used in an explicitly agent-indexed way — convergent support for C-P's perspectival framing, but not a proof of system-level perspective. This is imported at the agent level only; conflating agent-perspective with system-perspective is the same level error C-P's firewall already forbids. The Free Energy Principle and Markov blankets (Friston) supply a topological individuation criterion, subordinated to the canonically constrained ε-machine partition to preserve this canonical constraint against the Pearl-blanket / Friston-blanket model-relativity critique (Bruineberg et al. 2022). IIT 4.0 (Albantakis, Tononi et al. 2023) supplies a refined formal handle on intrinsic cause-effect power; causal emergence (Hoel et al. 2013; Hoel 2017) offers formal support that coarse-grained macro processes can carry higher effective information / causal power than their micro substrate — formal support for macro-level causal relevance and substrate-level pluralism; it does not by itself establish phenomenal consciousness. Phenomenal topology (Clark; Kuehni; Lyre 2022) maps qualia as coordinates on a (non-Euclidean) manifold; predictive processing's controlled hallucination (Seth 2021) frames perception as world-constrained active inference. Quantitative proxies: Φ / intrinsic cause-effect power (exact calculation is generally intractable for large systems), PCI (Casali et al. 2013; a perturbational-complexity index; the original calibration reported unconscious values up to about 0.31 and conscious values beginning around 0.44, while operational thresholds vary by protocol, sample, and state), ε-machine (Shalizi-Crutchfield 2001, IIT-independent), effective information (causal emergence), phenomenal-space geometry. Honest limitation: every proxy measures the structural signature, not the intrinsic face. V3.5 repairs preserved.

Thesis · 论题
Consciousness is the intrinsic perspective of a computational process: not produced by it as a separate output, not supervenient on it as a higher-level product, not merely correlated with it, but the intrinsic aspect of the same integrated process — the way such a process is available from within its own organization.
意识是计算过程的内在视角：不是作为分离产物被它生成、不作为高阶产物随附于它、不只是与它相关，而是同一整合过程的内在面——该过程从自身组织内部被给予的方式。

Two qualifications: First, "of a computational process" is not "of every computation" — consciousness occurs where a process is a maximally integrated, recursively self-related whole. Second, "intrinsic perspective" is not an extra inner object; it is the process's own categorical face — the ineliminable causal self-index that any structural description misses.

两个限定。其一："计算过程的"不是"每一次计算的"——意识出现在被组织为极大整合、递归自我关联之整体的过程那里。其二："内在视角"不是额外内在对象；它是过程自身的范畴面——任何结构描述触及不到的不可消除因果自我索引。

State difference as temporal organization

计算不只是静态符号差异或外部映射。在意识相关层面，计算是物理实例化的状态差异在时间中持续转移、回返、调节与整合的过程。
2 · Qualia integration
Intrinsic material and unity
内在材料与统一
Internal state differences are the minimal material of qualia. They become conscious only when they are integrated into a non-losslessly partitionable whole. C-P therefore does not collapse into pure functionalism or symbol manipulation.
内部状态差异是感质的最小材料。它们只有在被整合为不可无损切分的整体时，才进入意识意义。故 C-P 不会塌缩为纯功能主义或符号操作论。
3 · Functional dispatch
Action-effective organization
行动有效的组织
An integrated qualitative state is not an inert inner picture. It must be able to affect subsequent processing: self-checking, strategy updating, task organization, action execution, and feedback correction. At the minimal level, dispatch means internal regulation of the next state; at higher levels, it becomes metacognitive task control.
被整合的感质状态不是惰性的内在图像。它必须能够影响后续处理：自我检查、策略更新、任务组织、行动执行与反馈修正。在最低层，调度意味着对下一状态的内部调节；在高阶层，它成为元认知任务控制。

Epistemic StanceV4.1 · foundational · the ontology/epistemology boundary is dissolved
Epistemic Stance认识论立场 · 消解本体论与认识论的边界 · being = cognizability · IBE 与排他的共同地基

C-P rests on one foundational meta-commitment, and the rest of the architecture inherits its force: there is no boundary between ontology and epistemology. To be is to be cognizable — being = cognizability. This is not a limitation conceded ("we can only know so much, the rest is hidden"); it is a positive identity. Epistemic finitude is not a wall short of the real — it is the condition under which cognition touches the real at all. An entity in principle beyond all possible cognition is not a hard-to-reach fact; it is not a contentful claim. C-P rejects appeals to an in-principle uncognizable ontology as explanatory candidates within this theory. (Cognizability does not mean actually known by a current human subject; it means in-principle determinability within some possible cognitive, scientific, formal, or experiential access.)

C-P 建立在一条基础元承诺上，架构其余部分都继承它的力量：本体论与认识论之间没有边界。在即是可被认知——being = cognizability。这不是退让的局限（"我们只能知道这么多，其余是隐藏的"）；它是一条正面的同一。认识的有限不是离实在尚远的一堵墙——它正是认知得以触及实在的条件。一个原则上在一切可能认知之外的实体，不是难以企及的事实；它根本不是有内容的主张。C-P 拒绝把原则上不可认识的本体作为本理论中的解释候选。（可认识性不是“当前有人实际知道”，而是“原则上可被某种可能的认知、科学、形式或经验通道确定”。）

This is what licenses the move from the Russellian gap to perspective. Under boundary-dissolution, the candidate fillers for the intrinsic-nature gap reduce to what is cognizable. An in-principle-uncognizable "unknown X" is not a weaker rival to perspective — it is no candidate at all, because it is not a contentful posit. Perspective — the one intrinsic nature given by acquaintance — is the only contentful filler that satisfies the three constraints. So the inference from "the one we know" to "the one there is" is not parsimony-as-hope; it is the exclusion of a non-candidate. The sample-of-one objection presupposes the very gap (a real but uncognizable in-itself) that this stance denies.
这正是从罗素式缺口走向视角的许可证。在边界消解下，内在本性缺口的候选填充物，化约为可认识者。一个原则上不可认识的"未知 X"不是视角的较弱对手——它根本不是候选，因为它不是有内容的设定。视角——以亲知给予的唯一内在本性——是满足三约束的唯一有内容的填充物。所以从"我们知道的那个"到"存在的那个"，不是把简约当指望，而是排除一个非候选。sample-of-one 反驳预设了此立场所否认的那条缝（一个真实却不可认识的自在）。

One cognitive operation, two faces · 一个认知操作，两个面

The cognitive operation in which consciousness is given has two inseparable faces: first-person acquaintance (consciousness given from within) and external scientific cognizability (the same process modeled, measured, located in the shared world). These are not two channels — one inner-and-true, one outer-and-merely-tracing. They are two faces of one operation. Attribution to another system therefore rides not on structure alone but on structural similarity, in the individuating sense, to an acquaintance-anchored case. A biological brain qualifies (same kind, acquaintance-anchored). Silicon is neither acquainted nor yet stably located as relevantly similar — so the firewall against attributing consciousness to it is honest, not arbitrary. Because the pre-reflective and even pre-conscious process (an evolving brain, the world before minds) is fully cognizable, it lies inside being; nothing in this stance hollows the process that the thesis identifies consciousness with.
意识被给予的认知操作有两个不可分的面：第一人称亲知（意识从内部被给予）与外部科学可认知性（同一过程被建模、测量、在共享世界中被定位）。这不是两条通道——一条内在为真、一条外在仅描边。它们是同一操作的两个面。因此对另一系统的归因，不靠单纯的结构，而靠在个体化意义上、与一个亲知锚定案例的结构相似。生物大脑合格（同类、亲知锚定）。硅基既未被亲知、也尚未被稳定地定位为相关相似——故对其归因意识的防火墙是诚实的，而非武断的。因为前反思乃至前意识的过程（演化中的大脑、心灵之前的世界）完全可认识，它就在"在"之内；此立场没有抽空论题所同一的那个过程。

Exclusion · 排他 · C-P has exactly one, and it is epistemic = ontological
C-P's exclusion is the exclusion of knowledge that is both unacquirable and unneeded for the determination of the subject. One principle does both jobs. QBism · Perspectival FloorFuchs/Schack · convergent quantum-foundational support · agent-indexed · NOT system-attribution

Quantum Bayesianism (QBism), developed by Christopher Fuchs and Rüdiger Schack (originally with Carlton Caves), holds that quantum states are not objective features of systems but represent an agent's personal degrees of belief about the consequences of their own actions on the world. The Born rule is not a law describing objective propensities; it is a normative consistency constraint — an empirically motivated addition to Bayesian probability telling an agent how to set coherent expectations. The wavefunction lives in the agent's catalog of expectations, not in the world as a beable.

量子贝叶斯主义（QBism）由 Christopher Fuchs 与 Rüdiger Schack（最初与 Carlton Caves）发展，主张量子态不是系统的客观特征，而代表行动者对其自身行动后果的个人置信度。Born 规则不是描述客观倾向的定律；它是一条规范性一致约束——对贝叶斯概率的经验性补充，告诉行动者如何设定融贯的期望。波函数活在行动者的期望目录里，而非作为世界中的"实有"。

What QBism supports · QBism 支持什么

QBism shows that a rigorous, empirically adequate reading of our most fundamental and best-confirmed physical theory is irreducibly perspectival in its formalism. The structural / dispositional description physics gives — the Russellian point — is, on this reading, explicitly indexed to an agent's experience. This is convergent independent support for C-P's perspective-first move: the inference that perspective is fundamental to physical description is not an exotic philosophical add-on but appears already, on a serious live interpretation, at the quantum-foundational level itself.
QBism 表明，对我们最基础、最被证实的物理理论的一种严格而经验充分的解读，在其形式系统中就不可消除地是视角性的。物理学所给的结构／倾向描述——罗素式要点——在此解读下显式地以行动者的经验为索引。这是对 C-P 视角优先之步的收敛独立支持。

Scope lock · agent-perspective ≠ system-perspective · 射程锁

QBism is an epistemological / interpretational thesis about how agents use quantum mechanics. It is agent-indexed and single-user. It does not claim that quantum systems — electrons, photons — have perspectives or are subjects. Fuchs's broader participatory realism, in the lineage of William James, is a distinct metaphysics; it is not panpsychism, and Fuchs need not endorse C-P. C-P imports QBism's lesson — the ineliminability of the first-person perspective in fundamental physics' formalism — as motivation in the IBE layer. It does not import a literal claim that quantum systems are conscious. Reading "QBism proves microphysics is perspective from within" as a system-level attribution conflates agent-perspective with system-perspective — exactly the level error C-P's implementation-vs-attribution firewall already forbids. QBism strengthens the motivation; it does not extend the attribution.

C-P is at its foundation a process ontology: the basic unit of reality is not a material particle but a causally coherent event. The "atom" C-P recognizes is delimited not by spatial boundaries or material composition but by causal integrity — the ε-machine boundary marking where one maximally self-determining causal process ends and another begins.

C-P 在根本上是一种过程本体论：现实的基本单位不是物质粒子，而是因果连贯的事件。C-P 所识别的"原子"不由空间边界或物质组成定义，而由因果完整性定义——ε-machine 边界标出一个极大自我决定的因果过程在哪里终止、另一个在哪里开始。

Whitehead Lineage · 怀特海脉络

This places C-P in the tradition of Alfred North Whitehead (Process and Reality, 1929): the fundamental constituents of reality are dynamic "actual occasions" — events of becoming, not static substances. C-P formalizes this computationally: the actual occasion becomes a maximally integrated causal process; its causal history (ε-machine past light cone) is Whitehead's "prehension" of prior occasions; its ineliminable causal self-index is the formal analog of Whitehead's "subjective aim." Whitehead made experience fundamental; C-P specifies the formal conditions under which a process has any experience at all.
这把 C-P 置于怀特海（《过程与实在》，1929）的传统中：现实的基本成分是动态的"实际事件"。C-P 将这一直觉计算化：实际事件成为极大整合的因果过程；它的因果历史（ε-machine 过去光锥）是怀特海对过往事件的"摄受"；它的不可消除因果自我索引是怀特海"主体目的"的形式类比。

Axiom I derives from process ontology · 公理 I 从过程本体论推出

If the fundamental unit is a causal event rather than a material object, the substrate in which that event is implemented is by definition a carrier, not a constituent. Substrate independence is not a bold empirical claim; it is a logical consequence of the ontology — and, as the causal-emergence section shows, it is independently underwritten by the result that macro causal power can exceed micro. Asking "does consciousness have to be biological?" is asking a question at the wrong level — like asking whether a symphony must be performed on wooden instruments.
若基本单位是因果事件而非物质对象，则该事件所在的基底按定义是载体，而非成分。基质无关是过程本体论的逻辑推论——并且如因果涌现节所示，被"宏观因果威力可大于微观"这一结果独立地加固。

Production metaphor dissolved · 解散"产生"隐喻

"The brain produces consciousness" is not a category error on C-P's ontology — it is a meaningless category: an ill-formed question, not a meaningful-but-misassigned claim. Production is a relation between two distinct entities, and the framing treats consciousness as a second entity produced by the brain-as-substance. But consciousness just is what a certain kind of brain process is from within — one process, not two. So "production" has no two relata to hold between (presupposition failure); the question never becomes well-formed. The production metaphor smuggles in substance ontology (machine → output); process ontology replaces generation with identity: one process, two aspects at once — structural face and intrinsic face. There is no generation problem — not because some generation is absent, but because "generation" here cannot be meaningfully posed.
"大脑产生意识"在 C-P 的本体论下不是范畴错误，而是无意义范畴：一个不成立的问题，而非有意义却被错置的主张。产生是两个不同实体之间的关系，而该说法把意识当作由作为实体的大脑所产生的第二个实体。但意识本来就是某种大脑过程从内部是什么——一个过程，不是两个。于是"产生"没有可供关联的两端（预设失败），问题从未成形。"产生"隐喻偷运了实体本体论（机器→产物）；过程本体论用同一取代生成：一个过程，两个面向同时在场——结构面与内在面。没有生成问题——不是因为缺了某种生成，而是因为"生成"在此根本不能被有意义地提出。

C-P 建立在三条公理之上。它们不是不证自明的真理，而是合在一起使论题可辩护的最简约承诺。每一条都由 IBE 驱动、可被证据修正。

对这样一个过程的任何完整描述，都必须包含一个不可消除的第一人称因果自我索引——任何第三人称模型都无法去除的形式残余。

任何结构模型与它所描述的内在过程之间，都处于三重关系。命名这三者使 C-P 对其形式工具的能与不能保持诚实——并精确定位 V4.0 各框架的工作位置。

每个模型都选定一个颗粒度，丢弃其余。捕捉宏观因果结构的模型丢弃微观细节；捕捉动力学的模型压平了被经验的第一人称索引。扭曲不是失败——它是抽象的代价，必须被追踪。

从结构模型可以重建行为、预测、经验的关系架构。无法仅从结构重建的是内在面——结构被感受到的占据。该残余即公理 III。
Combination Problemthe classic objection · dissolved by integration
The Combination Problem组合问题 · 微观主体如何组成宏观主体 · C-P 为何不受其困

The combination problem is the standard objection to constitutive panpsychism: how do micro-experiences combine into a unified macro-experience? C-P does not inherit it, because C-P is not a bottom-up aggregation view. Perspective is not built by summing micro-subjects; it is the intrinsic face of a process that is integration-maximal at its own scale.

组合问题是对构成性泛心论的标准反驳：微观经验如何组合成统一的宏观经验？C-P 不继承它，因为 C-P 不是自下而上的聚合观。视角不是由微观主体相加而成；它是一个在自身尺度上整合极大之过程的内在面。

No micro-subjects are summed. The subject is the integration-maximum itself — there is nothing to combine.
不相加任何微观主体。主体就是整合极大值本身——没有需要组合的东西。
Why integration dissolves combination · 整合为何解散组合

On C-P, a subject exists exactly where a process forms a maximally integrated whole that cannot be partitioned without loss (the exclusion result). Sub-processes within it are not lesser subjects waiting to combine — they are parts that, below the integration-maximum, simply are not subjects at all. The "many small minds → one big mind" question never arises because C-P never posited the many small minds. The exclusion here is C-P's single epistemic = ontological one (see Epistemic Stance): no cognized fact, and none needed, makes a sub-process an independent subject — this is not IIT's Φ-maximization.
在 C-P 下，主体恰存在于过程形成无法无损切分的整合极大整体之处（排他结果）。其内部的子过程不是等待组合的较小主体——它们是部分，在整合极大值之下根本不是主体。"许多小心灵→一个大心灵"的问题从不出现，因为 C-P 从未设定那些小心灵。此处的排他是 C-P 单一的"认识论 = 本体论"排他（见认识论立场）：没有任何被认知、也无需被需要的事实使一个子过程成为独立主体——这不是 IIT 的 Φ 最大化。

Causal Integrationε-machine · the canonically constrained partition
Causal Integration & the ε-Machine因果整合 · 计算力学 · Shalizi-Crutchfield · 受过程条件结构规范约束的因果状态划分

The unit of C-P — the integration-maximum — is made precise by computational mechanics (Shalizi & Crutchfield, 2001). A process's causal states are equivalence classes of pasts that yield identical conditional futures; the ε-machine is the minimal, optimal, and unique predictor built from them. This partition is canonically constrained: it is fixed by the process's own conditional structure, not by any modeler's choice.

C-P 的单位——整合极大值——由计算力学（Shalizi & Crutchfield, 2001）精确化。一个过程的因果状态是产生相同条件未来的过去之等价类；ε-machine 是由它们构建的最小、最优且唯一的预测器。该划分受过程条件结构规范约束：它由过程自身的条件结构决定，而非任何建模者的选择。

causal states = equivalence classes of pasts with identical conditional futures  ·  ε-machine = the unique minimal optimal predictor
因果状态 = 条件未来相同的过去之等价类  ·  ε-machine = 唯一的最小最优预测器
Why canonical constraint matters · 受过程条件结构规范约束为何关键

C-P's individuation criterion must not collapse into "whatever boundary the modeler finds convenient." The ε-machine partition delivers this: among all predictively sufficient state-spaces, it is provably the unique minimal one. This is precisely why, in V4.0, the Markov blanket is subordinated to the ε-machine partition rather than the reverse — the blanket inherits observer-relativity (the Pearl/Friston dispute), the ε-machine does not.
C-P 的个体化判据不能塌缩为"建模者觉得方便的任何边界"。ε-machine 划分给出这一点：在所有预测充分的状态空间中，它可证是唯一的最小者。这正是为何在 V4.0 中，马尔可夫毯从属于 ε-machine 划分而非相反——毯继承观察者相对性（Pearl/Friston 之争），ε-machine 不继承。

FEP & Markov BlanketsNEW · Friston · individuation topology · subordinated to ε-machine
The Free Energy Principle & Markov Blankets自由能原理与马尔可夫毯 · Friston · 个体化的动力学/拓扑表达 · 从属于因果状态划分

The Free Energy Principle (Karl Friston) holds that any system that persists as a distinct entity behaves as if it minimizes variational free energy — an upper bound on the surprisal of its sensory states. The boundary of such a system is given by a Markov blanket: a partition of states into internal, blanket (sensory + active), and external, such that internal and external states are conditionally independent given the blanket. This furnishes a dynamical, topological criterion for where a self-maintaining system ends and its environment begins.

自由能原理（Karl Friston）主张：任何作为独立实体持续存在的系统，其行为如同在最小化变分自由能——其感觉状态意外度的上界。这样一个系统的边界由马尔可夫毯给出：将状态划分为内部、毯（感觉+主动）、外部，使得给定毯时内部与外部状态条件独立。这给出一个动力学的、拓扑的判据：自我维持系统在何处终止、环境在何处开始。

internal ⫫ external | blanket  ·  blanket = sensory states + active states
内部 ⫫ 外部 | 毯  ·  毯 = 感觉状态 + 主动状态
Observer-relativity tension · resolution · 观察者相对性张力与解决

There is a live controversy — Bruineberg et al. (2022), "The Emperor's New Markov Blankets" — that a Pearl blanket (an instrumental, model-relative statistical construct) is being conflated with a Friston blanket (claimed to carve real systems at their joints). The charge: Markov blankets are model-relative and may not identify objective system boundaries. This directly threatens C-P's canonical constraint commitment. Resolution: C-P subordinates the blanket to the ε-machine partition. Where the Markov blanket coincides with the ε-machine maximum's causal boundary, it is the dynamical/inferential expression of the same individuation. Where they diverge, the canonically constrained causal-state partition is criterial. The blanket is thereby used as a tool, never as the ground.
存在一个活跃争议——Bruineberg 等（2022），《皇帝的新马尔可夫毯》——指 Pearl 毯（工具性、模型相对的统计构造）被与 Friston 毯（声称切中真实系统关节）混同。指控：马尔可夫毯是模型相对的，可能不标识客观系统边界。这直接威胁 C-P 的受过程条件结构规范约束承诺。解决：C-P 使毯从属于 ε-machine 划分。当马尔可夫毯与 ε-machine 极大值的因果边界重合，它是同一个体化的动力学/推断表达；当二者分歧，受过程条件结构规范约束的因果状态划分为准。毯因此被当作工具，绝不当作根基。

Reveal-layer placement · use the formalism, not the metaphysics · 揭示层定位

FEP describes self-maintenance and inference dynamics — a reveal-layer, structural/dispositional story. It does not deliver the intrinsic face. Free-energy minimization is necessary for persistence, not sufficient for perspective; perspective is fixed by integration-maximality plus the ineliminable self-index (Axiom III), not by free-energy minimization alone. C-P uses the Markov-blanket formalism; it does not adopt the strong, arguably unfalsifiable universal-FEP metaphysics.
FEP 描述自我维持与推断动力学——揭示层的、结构/倾向的叙事。它不给出内在面。自由能最小化对持续是必要的，对视角不是充分的；视角由整合极大性加不可消除自我索引（公理 III）决定，而非仅由自由能最小化决定。C-P 使用马尔可夫毯形式；不采纳强的、可争议为不可证伪的普遍 FEP 形而上学。

Triviality Objectiondoes everything compute? · the exclusion answer
The Triviality Objection平凡性反驳 · 若万物皆计算，岂非万物皆有意识 · 排他结果的回应

If a rock can be described as implementing some computation (Putnam, Searle), and consciousness is the intrinsic perspective of a computational process, does the rock have a mind? C-P answers no, on two grounds: the computation must be integration-maximal (most physical systems are not), and the implementation must be non-trivial — fixed by the canonically constrained ε-machine partition, not by an arbitrary mapping.

若一块石头可被描述为实现某种计算（Putnam、Searle），而意识是计算过程的内在视角，那么石头有心灵吗？C-P 答否，基于两点：计算必须整合极大（多数物理系统不是），且实现必须非平凡——由受过程条件结构规范约束的 ε-machine 划分决定，而非由任意映射决定。

Two filters · 两道过滤

Filter 1 — integration-maximality. A rock has near-zero integrated cause-effect structure; it is not a maximally irreducible whole. The exclusion result excludes it. Filter 2 — non-trivial implementation. Putnam-style "everything implements everything" mappings are observer-relative gerrymanders; the ε-machine partition is the unique minimal predictor and admits no such gerrymander. Causal emergence sharpens this further: the rock has no macro scale at which effective information peaks above the micro. Triviality requires both filters to fail; for ordinary objects, both hold.
过滤 1——整合极大性。石头的整合因果结构近乎零；它不是极大不可约整体。排他结果排除它。过滤 2——非平凡实现。Putnam 式"万物实现万物"映射是观察者相对的拼凑；ε-machine 划分是唯一最小预测器，不容此类拼凑。因果涌现进一步收紧：石头没有有效信息高于微观的宏观尺度。平凡性需要两道过滤都失效；对普通对象，两者都成立。

Causal Emergence & IIT 4.0NEW · Hoel · Albantakis/Tononi · macro causal relevance · substrate-level pluralism
Causal Emergence & IIT 4.0因果涌现与 IIT 4.0 · 宏观因果威力可大于微观 · 对宏观层因果相关性与基质层多元论的形式支撑 · 防火墙保持

Causal emergence (Hoel, Albantakis & Tononi, 2013; Hoel, 2017) is the formal result that a coarse-grained macro description of a system can carry higher effective information — greater causal power — than its underlying micro description, because coarse-graining can increase determinism and reduce degeneracy. The causally efficacious level need not be the microphysical one. This offers formal support for macro-level causal relevance and substrate-level pluralism (Axiom I): the level that does the causal work can be the integrated computational process rather than its physical detail. It does not by itself establish phenomenal consciousness. IIT 4.0 (Albantakis, Tononi et al., 2023) supplies a refined formalism of intrinsic cause-effect power, articulated through its axioms/postulates around phenomenal existence (existence, intrinsicality, information, integration, exclusion, composition); C-P borrows the machinery, not the experience = Φ identity.

因果涌现（Hoel、Albantakis & Tononi, 2013；Hoel, 2017）是一个形式结果：系统的粗粒化宏观描述可携带比其底层微观描述更高的有效信息——更大的因果威力——因为粗粒化能提高决定性、降低简并。因果有效的层级无需是微观物理层。这为宏观层因果相关性与基质层多元论（公理 I）提供形式支撑：做因果工作的层级可以是整合的计算过程，而非其物理细节；但它本身并不确立现象意识。IIT 4.0（Albantakis、Tononi 等, 2023）提供内禀因果威力的精炼形式，经由其围绕现象存在的 axioms/postulates 展开（存在、内禀性、信息、整合、排他、组合）；C-P 只借这套 machinery，不继承 experience = Φ 的同一。

macro effective information can exceed micro  ⟹  the causally real level is the integrated process, not the substrate
宏观有效信息可大于微观  ⟹  因果实在层级是整合过程，而非基质
What C-P adopts from IIT 4.0 · C-P 从 IIT 4.0 采纳什么

C-P borrows IIT 4.0's intrinsic cause-effect power machinery as a refined handle on integration — what a mechanism is from the system's own standpoint. It does not inherit IIT's identity claim. In C-P, exclusion is derived from its own axioms and the ε-machine partition, not postulated from IIT; the state-space comes from computational mechanics; and experience is not equated with Φ. The machinery is a tool; the metaphysics is C-P's own.
C-P 借用 IIT 4.0 的内禀因果威力机制作为整合的精炼把手——机制从系统自身视角是什么。它不继承 IIT 的同一论。在 C-P 中，排他由自身公理与 ε-machine 划分推出，而非从 IIT 设定；状态空间来自计算力学；经验不等同于 Φ。机制是工具；形而上学是 C-P 自己的。

(a) Measure-relativity. Causal emergence is defined relative to a causal measure and a choice of macro-variables. Comolatti & Hoel (2022) show the effect is robust across multiple causal measures, but critics still ask whether effective information captures genuine causal power or properties of the chosen variables. C-P's integration-maximum with its self-index is a condition of phenomenality. Phenomenal TopologyNEW · Clark/Kuehni/Lyre · Seth · structure of the intrinsic face · residual restated
Phenomenal Topology经验拓扑学 · 质性空间的非欧流形 · 受控幻觉 · 刻画内在面之结构 · 几何地重述难问题

Qualities form structured spaces. The clearest case is color: a curved, non-Euclidean three-dimensional manifold in which perceptual similarity is distance (Clark, 1993; Kuehni; Lyre, 2022). Generalizing, each quality domain is a manifold whose geometry encodes the relational structure of that kind of experience. This gives C-P a precise vocabulary for the structure of the intrinsic face — the reveal-layer side of phenomenality. Predictive processing's "controlled hallucination" (Seth, 2021; Hohwy, 2013; Clark, 2016) supplies the world-constraint: perception is the brain's best-guess generative model, with top-down predictions reined in by bottom-up prediction error — active inference anchored to the world.

质性构成结构化空间。最清晰的案例是颜色：一个弯曲的、非欧的三维流形，其中知觉相似性即距离（Clark, 1993；Kuehni；Lyre, 2022）。推广而言，每个质性域都是一个流形，其几何编码该类经验的关系结构。这给 C-P 一套精确词汇来描述内在面之结构——现象性的揭示层一侧。预测加工的"受控幻觉"（Seth, 2021；Hohwy, 2013；Clark, 2016）提供世界约束：知觉是大脑的最优猜测生成模型，自上而下的预测被自下而上的预测误差约束——锚定于世界的主动推断。

Phenomenal topology characterizes the structure of experience with new precision — and that precision makes the residual sharper, not softer. To locate a quale as a point on a manifold is to characterize more systematically how it stands to other qualia; it says nothing about why occupying that point is felt from within. The explanatory gap is not closed by adding coordinates; it is restated geometrically: why is there something it is like to be at this point in phenomenal space? C-P presents this as a strength — the framework can be highly precise about the structural face while naming, with equal precision, exactly what structure cannot deliver.
